# Review Walkthrough Notes

These notes support the live demo of the pull request review flow.

## What we cover

- Opening the Files changed tab and reading the diff
- Starting a draft review
- Leaving line comments on the diff
- Discarding a draft review when you change your mind
- Submitting an approving review

## Things to try during the session

1. Look at the diff for this file.
2. Start a new review in the top-right of the review panel.
3. Add a couple of line comments.
4. Choose **Submit** to publish the review, or drop the review to discard it.

> Reminder: this is a throwaway sandbox. Anything the team does here is fine.
